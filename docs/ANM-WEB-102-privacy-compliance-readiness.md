# ANM-WEB-102 Privacy & Compliance Readiness

This document describes technical readiness, not legal certification.

Readiness controls:

- Data inventory: public content, contact/newsletter, consent, analytics, audit, media, secrets.
- Data minimization: public DTO safety, analytics minimization, no full-song/public private-media fields.
- Consent: ANM-WEB-101 policy, GPC/DNT behavior, withdrawal.
- Unsubscribe: hashed tokens, single-use confirmation/unsubscribe.
- Retention: model fields and runbook readiness; final legal retention schedule pending.
- Access controls: backend permissions and admin-only confidential data routes.
- Incident response: plan and playbooks included.
- Vendor inventory: see vendor security inventory.

Do not claim GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, or other compliance solely from this implementation. Legal counsel must review final obligations and public policy text.
