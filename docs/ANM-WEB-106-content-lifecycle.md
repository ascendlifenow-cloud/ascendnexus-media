# ANM-WEB-106 Content Lifecycle

`ContentLifecycleService` reconciles artists, releases, and gallery items into persistent lifecycle records. Stages include planned, draft, review, scheduled, published, optimize, stale, and archived.

Lifecycle records do not alter publication state. They provide an operational review layer for follow-up, optimization, stale-content detection, and long-term catalog care.

Optimization recommendations cover missing artwork, missing metadata, weak descriptions, duplicate SEO readiness, broken links, low engagement readiness, unused galleries, stale homepage content, inactive artists, orphaned media, storage optimization, CDN optimization, search optimization, and performance improvements.
