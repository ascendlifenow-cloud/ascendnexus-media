# ANM-WEB-107 Platform Analytics

The analytics collector stores platform metrics for verified uploads:

- Views
- Streams
- Likes
- Shares
- Comments
- Subscribers
- Followers
- CTR
- Watch time
- Retention
- Playlist adds
- Platform ranking
- Revenue readiness

Local connectors produce zero-value operational baselines. External connectors only collect real metrics after connector credentials are configured and uploads are verified.
